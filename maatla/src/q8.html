(a) and (b) · volts measure energy per coulomb

Energy the battery gives each coulomb, over the whole circuit (e.m.f.)
4.8 J/C = 4.8 V
Energy each coulomb transfers in the 12 Ω series resistor (p.d. on the voltmeter)

Current is how many coulombs pass each second. Voltage is how many joules each coulomb carries or gives up. E.m.f. is energy converted to electrical, for the whole circuit. P.d. is energy converted from electrical, across a component.
