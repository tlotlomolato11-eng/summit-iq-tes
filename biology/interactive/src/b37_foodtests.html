Check #2Q3.7(c), (d) · [2] [2]

Compare Benedict's on “a little glucose” and “a lot of glucose”. What does green mean? And why grind and dissolve the food first?

Mark points(d) Green suggests a smaller amount of reducing sugar than a sample that turns brick-red. The colour change (blue → green/yellow/orange → brick-red) roughly shows how much reducing sugar is present.
(c) Grinding and dissolving releases the substances being tested for and increases the surface area so the reagent can react properly. It also lets the mixture be handled and mixed as a liquid.
