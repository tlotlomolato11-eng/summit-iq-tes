The tube, and the organs beside it

Food only travels through the tube: mouth, oesophagus, stomach, small intestine, large intestine, rectum and anus. The salivary glands, liver, gall bladder and pancreas send secretions in but food never passes through them. Tap any organ, take the quiz, or follow a mouthful of food.

Follow the food. Does it ever enter the gall bladder? Where is digested food absorbed, and where is water taken back?

Mark points(b) The gall bladder only stores bile (made by the liver) and releases it into the duodenum. Food itself never passes through the gall bladder, so it is an associated (accessory) organ rather than part of the tube food travels along.
(c) (i) Small intestine (ileum). (ii) Large intestine (colon).
